# ShowHiddenChannels

Show channels that you do not have access to view.

Discord now strips the name, topic and permission overwrites of such channels on the server, before they are sent to your client. Those details cannot be recovered by any client.

What the plugin still shows:

- hidden channels in the channel list, at their real position and in their real category
- the real channel type, so voice, forum and stage channels are told apart
- who is currently connected to a hidden voice channel
